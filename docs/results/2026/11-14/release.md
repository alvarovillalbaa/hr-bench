## Limits

This public synthetic suite is inspectable and uncalibrated. Its primary
equal-family score and descriptive Wilson interval do not establish real-HR
competence, comparative superiority, independent human validation, a blind
holdout or global adoption. Other models/providers, real cohorts, physical
touch devices, Safari/Firefox and broad accessibility/performance remain
unverified. Imported model identities are self-reported; hashes bind content,
not identity. Tools only mutate fictional state; no real employment, invitation,
email or access revocation occurs. Offboarding creates approval requests while
accounts remain active. Domain calibration and independent users are the next
product gates. The broader portfolio goal remains active.
